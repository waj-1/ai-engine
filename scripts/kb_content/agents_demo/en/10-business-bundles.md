# Business Internet and Bundles

{{brand}} offers dedicated connectivity for shops, clinics, cafés and offices. All business fiber plans include static IPs, a 4-hour fault response and priority support.

## Business Fiber 500

- 500 Mbps business fiber
- 1 static IP
- 4-hour fault response and priority support
- Contract: 12 or 24 months
- **Best for:** shops, clinics and offices up to about 15 people.

## Business Fiber 1 Gig

- 1 Gbps business fiber
- 5 static IPs
- 4-hour fault response and priority support
- Contract: 12 or 24 months
- **Best for:** offices with 15 or more people, cloud-heavy teams and online shops.

## SME Business Bundle

Everything a small business needs on one bill:

- Business Fiber 500 (500 Mbps, 1 static IP, 4-hour fault response, priority support)
- 5 business mobile lines with 30 GB of data each
- A cloud phone number for the business
- Contract: 24 months
- **Best for:** cafés, salons, clinics and small offices with a small team on the move.

## 5G Backup for business

An add-on that switches your business to 5G automatically if the fiber drops, so card machines and the till stay online.

- Contract: 12 months
- **Best for:** any business that cannot afford to go offline.

## Which one fits?

- **A single shop or small clinic that mainly needs reliable internet:** Business Fiber 500.
- **A larger office, a team working in the cloud, or an online shop:** Business Fiber 1 Gig.
- **A small team that also needs mobile lines and a business phone number:** SME Business Bundle.
- **Card payments or bookings that must never stop:** add 5G Backup to any business fiber plan.

## Getting a callback from the business sales team

1. Call the business inquiries line, or use the business contact form on the website.
2. Share your business name, a contact name and mobile number, the business address or area, the number of staff, and the services you are interested in.
3. A member of the business sales team will call you back to check coverage at your address and prepare an offer.

Coverage depends on the business address, so the team always confirms fiber availability before an order is placed.
