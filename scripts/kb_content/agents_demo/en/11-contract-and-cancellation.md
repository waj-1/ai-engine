# Contracts, Pausing and Cancellation

This guide explains {{brand}} contract lengths, the early-termination fee, pausing your home service while you travel, and how to cancel.

## Contract lengths

| Service | Contract options |
|---|---|
| Home Fiber 300, 500 and 1 Gig | 12 or 24 months |
| Home 5G Wireless | No contract |
| Mobile Essential, Mobile Plus, Mobile Unlimited | No contract or 12 months |
| Mesh Wi-Fi add-on, Family line add-on | No contract |
| Business Fiber 500 and 1 Gig | 12 or 24 months |
| SME Business Bundle | 24 months |
| 5G Backup for business | 12 months |

On home fiber plans, choosing a 24-month contract lowers the monthly price by 20 dirhams in the UAE or 20 riyals in Saudi Arabia.

## Early-termination fee

If you cancel a contract before it ends, the early-termination fee is 50% of the remaining monthly fees on the contract.

**Example:** you pay 349 dirhams a month and 6 months remain. The remaining monthly fees are 6 x 349 = 2,094 dirhams, so the early-termination fee is 1,047 dirhams.

Services with no contract have no early-termination fee.

## Pausing your home service for travel

If you will be away, you can pause your home service instead of cancelling it.

- You can pause for up to 3 months per year.
- The pause fee is 25 dirhams a month in the UAE or 25 riyals a month in Saudi Arabia.
- You keep your contract and your equipment.
- Your contract end date moves later by the number of months you paused.

**How to pause:**

1. Request the pause in the app or by calling support.
2. Choose the start date and how many months (up to 3).
3. Your service resumes automatically at the end of the pause.

## Cancelling at the end of your contract

There is no fee to cancel when your contract has ended. Contact support or use the app to request the cancellation.

## Moving to an area without fiber

If you move to an address where fiber is not available, you can cancel your home fiber service without the early-termination fee. You can also switch to Home 5G Wireless instead. See the "Moving House" guide for details.

## Returning equipment

When your home service is cancelled, return the router and any mesh units within 14 days of the cancellation.

## How to cancel

1. Contact support or use the app. You will be asked to verify your identity.
2. The agent confirms whether an early-termination fee applies and how much it is.
3. Agree the cancellation date.
4. Return your equipment within 14 days.

Your mobile lines are separate from your home service. Cancelling one does not cancel the other unless you ask.
