# Router and Wi-Fi Troubleshooting

Slow or no internet at home? Most problems can be fixed in a few minutes. Follow these steps in order before booking a technician from {{brand}}.

## Step 1: Check for an area outage

Before troubleshooting your own equipment, check whether there is a known outage in your area. Open the app or the website, or call support. If there is an outage, you do not need to do anything: our engineers are already working on it and support can tell you the expected fix time.

## Step 2: Check the lights

Look at both the fiber box (the small box where the fiber cable enters your home) and the router.

- **Red LOS light on the fiber box:** LOS means "loss of signal". The fiber signal to your home is lost. Restarting will not fix this; a technician visit is needed. Contact support to book one.
- **Fiber box normal, but no internet light on the router:** restart the router (Step 3).
- **All lights normal:** the connection is working. The issue is likely Wi-Fi coverage or too many devices (Steps 4 and 5).

## Step 3: Restart the router properly

1. Switch the router off at the power button or unplug it.
2. Wait 30 seconds.
3. Switch it back on and wait a few minutes for the lights to settle.
4. Test your connection again.

## Step 4: Use the right Wi-Fi network

Your router broadcasts two networks:

- **5 GHz:** faster, best for streaming, video calls and gaming. Works best in the same room or nearby rooms.
- **2.4 GHz:** reaches further but is slower. Too many devices on 2.4 GHz slows everything down.

Connect TVs, consoles and laptops to 5 GHz where possible, and leave 2.4 GHz for smart-home devices and far rooms.

## Step 5: Place the router well

- Keep it in an open, central spot in your home, raised off the floor.
- Do not put it inside cabinets or behind the TV.
- Keep it away from microwaves and thick walls.

## Step 6: Mesh units for bigger homes

If some rooms always have weak Wi-Fi, mesh units extend coverage across the home.

- **Home Fiber 1 Gig** already includes 2 mesh units with the router.
- On any other home plan, add the **Mesh Wi-Fi add-on**: one extra mesh unit rented for 25 dirhams a month in the UAE or 25 riyals a month in Saudi Arabia.
