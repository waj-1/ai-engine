# Moving House: Relocating Your Home Internet

If you are moving to a new home, {{brand}} can move your home internet service with you. This guide explains the cost, the timeline and what happens if the new address does not have fiber yet.

## Is relocation free?

Yes, once per contract year. Each contract year includes 1 free relocation of your home service. Your plan, price and contract stay the same after the move.

## How long does it take?

A relocation takes 3 to 5 working days from the date it is booked. To avoid being without internet, give us at least 5 working days notice before your moving date.

## Step 1: Check coverage at the new address

Before you book the move, check what is available at the new address:

1. Open the app or the website and use the coverage check, or call support.
2. Enter the new address or area.
3. You will see whether fiber, Home 5G Wireless, or both are available there.

## If the new address has fiber

1. Request the relocation in the app or by calling support, at least 5 working days before you move.
2. Choose a technician visit window at the new address: 9 am to 12 noon, 1 pm to 4 pm, or 4 pm to 7 pm.
3. Take your router and any mesh units with you. The technician connects them at the new home.
4. Your service at the old address stops when the move is completed.

## If the new address has no fiber

You have two options, and neither one carries the early-termination fee:

- **Switch to Home 5G Wireless.** Plug-and-play 5G home internet with typical speeds up to 250 Mbps, no installation visit and no contract. It is delivered the next working day. The price is 249 dirhams a month in the UAE or 239 riyals a month in Saudi Arabia.
- **Cancel your home service.** Because fiber is not available at your new address, you can cancel without the early-termination fee. Please return the equipment within 14 days.

If fiber is planned for your new area, support can tell you the planned date where one has been announced.

## Mobile lines are not affected

Moving house does not change your mobile lines. Your numbers, plans and SIM or eSIM keep working as normal, and you do not need to do anything for them.
