# Coverage: Fiber and 5G Wireless

## Overview

{{brand}} provides home internet in the UAE and Saudi Arabia using two technologies. Which one you can get depends on the area where you live.

## Fiber vs 5G wireless

- **Fiber:** a cable connection installed by a technician. Home fiber plans offer 300 Mbps, 500 Mbps or 1 Gbps with unlimited data, on a 12- or 24-month contract.
- **5G wireless:** a plug-and-play device that connects over the 5G network, with typical speeds up to 250 Mbps. There is no installation visit and no contract, and it is delivered the next working day.

If fiber is not yet available in your area, Home 5G Wireless is available in every area listed below.

## How to check coverage

- Check your address in the app or on the website.
- Or contact {{brand}} support and give your city and area name.

If your area is not listed above, {{brand}} does not currently confirm service there.

## Outages

Service outages and planned maintenance are announced by area. If your service stops working, check the app or contact support to see whether there is a known outage in your area, along with its status and expected fix time, before booking a technician visit.
