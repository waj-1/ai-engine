# Installation and Setup

## Overview

{{brand}} offers two kinds of home internet in the UAE and Saudi Arabia: fiber, which needs a technician visit, and Home 5G Wireless, which you set up yourself. This article explains what to expect for each.

## Fiber installation timeline

- Fiber is installed within 2 to 5 working days of your order.
- You choose a visit window when booking:
  - Morning: 9 am to 12 noon
  - Afternoon: 1 pm to 4 pm
  - Evening: 4 pm to 7 pm
- You can reschedule through the app or by contacting support.

## Who must be home

An adult must be at home for the whole technician visit to give access, agree where the equipment goes and confirm the service is working.

## Installation fee

- **12- or 24-month contract:** installation is free.
- **Without a contract:** a one-time installation fee of 200 dirhams in the UAE or 200 riyals in Saudi Arabia applies.

## What the technician does

During the visit the technician:

- Connects the fiber line to your home.
- Installs and sets up the Wi-Fi 6 router (and the 2 mesh units included with Home Fiber 1 Gig).
- Checks that the connection is working and runs a speed test.
- Helps you connect your first device and shows you the Wi-Fi name and password.

## Later technician visits

If you report a fault and a technician visit finds the problem is on the customer's side (for example your own cabling or devices) rather than the {{brand}} network, a visit fee of 150 dirhams or 150 riyals may apply.

## Home 5G Wireless delivery

Home 5G Wireless has no installation visit and no contract. The device is delivered the next working day.

## Home 5G Wireless self-setup

1. Place the device indoors near a window, away from thick walls and metal objects, for the best 5G signal.
2. Plug the device into power and switch it on.
3. Wait a few minutes until the indicator shows it is connected.
4. Connect your phone or laptop using the Wi-Fi name and password printed on the device label.
5. If the speed seems low, try another spot closer to a window.

If the device does not connect after these steps, contact {{brand}} support through the app or by phone.
