# SIM Cards and eSIM

This guide covers activating an eSIM, replacing a SIM, moving your number to a new phone, and what to do if your SIM is lost or stolen. It applies to all {{brand}} mobile lines.

## Does my phone support eSIM?

An eSIM is a digital SIM built into the phone. You can only use one if your phone supports eSIM. Check your phone's settings or the manufacturer's information before you request one. If your phone does not support eSIM, keep using a physical SIM.

## Activating an eSIM

1. Contact support or use the app and request an eSIM for your line.
2. Verify your identity (see below).
3. We send an eSIM QR code to the email address on file for your account.
4. On your phone, open the mobile or cellular settings, choose to add an eSIM, and scan the QR code.
5. Wait for the eSIM to activate. Your number, plan and balance stay the same.

**Important:** once the eSIM activates, your old physical SIM stops working.

## Identity verification for SIM changes

Every SIM swap or eSIM request needs ID verification. You will be asked for your account number or registered mobile, plus your date of birth or the last 4 digits of your national ID. This protects your number from being taken over by someone else.

## SIM and eSIM change fees

| Change | Fee |
|---|---|
| First SIM or eSIM change in a calendar year | Free |
| Each further change in the same calendar year | 25 dirhams (UAE) or 25 riyals (Saudi Arabia) |

A change includes replacing a physical SIM, switching from a physical SIM to an eSIM, or issuing a new eSIM.

## Moving your number to a new phone

Your number belongs to your account, not to the phone, so you can move it to a new device:

- **Physical SIM:** move the SIM card into the new phone. No change is needed.
- **eSIM:** request a new eSIM QR code for the new phone. This is issued as an eSIM change, so the fee table above applies.

## Lost or stolen SIM

Act quickly to protect your line:

1. **Block the SIM immediately.** Call support or use the app as soon as you notice the loss. Blocking stops anyone from using your number for calls, messages or data.
2. **Verify your identity** with the agent.
3. **Request a replacement** physical SIM or an eSIM. The fee table above applies.
4. Once the replacement is active, your number works again on your new SIM or eSIM.

## Quick tips

- Keep the email address on your account up to date, because eSIM QR codes are sent there.
- Do not share your eSIM QR code with anyone.
- Never share your account PIN or password with anyone, including people claiming to call from {{brand}}.
