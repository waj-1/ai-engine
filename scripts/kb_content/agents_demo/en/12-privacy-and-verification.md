# Privacy, Identity Verification and Safe Calls

{{brand}} protects your account by checking who we are speaking to before sharing or changing anything. This guide explains how verification works, what we will never ask for, and how to report a suspicious call.

## How we verify your identity

Before discussing your account, the agent asks for **two things**:

1. Your **account number** OR your **registered mobile number**, and
2. Your **date of birth** OR the **last 4 digits of your national ID**.

When we call you (for example about a bill or an offer), the agent first asks for your date of birth or the last 4 digits of your national ID before discussing anything.

## What we will never ask for

Our agents will never ask you for:

- Your card number
- Your PIN
- Your passwords

If anyone asks for these while claiming to be from {{brand}}, do not share them and end the call.

## Who can access your account

Account details are never shared with anyone who is not the account holder or an authorized user. This applies to family members, friends and colleagues too, unless they have been added as an authorized user.

## Adding an authorized user

Only the account holder can add an authorized user.

**In the app:**

1. Sign in to the app as the account holder.
2. Open your account settings and choose to add an authorized user.
3. Enter the person's details and confirm.

**By calling:**

1. Call support and verify your identity as the account holder.
2. Tell the agent the name and details of the person you want to add.
3. The agent confirms once the authorized user has been added.

## AI assistants and human agents

Your call may be handled by an AI assistant. It follows the same verification and privacy rules as our human agents. If you prefer to speak to a person, just ask and the call will be transferred to a human agent.

## Payment links

Payment links are only ever sent to the registered mobile number on your account. We will never send a payment link to a different number, and we never take card details over the phone.

## Reporting a suspicious call or message

If a call or message claiming to be from {{brand}} seems suspicious:

1. Do not share any personal or payment details.
2. End the call.
3. Contact {{brand}} support yourself using the contact details in the app or on the website.
4. Tell the agent what happened: the number that called you, the time, and what you were asked for.

If you think someone already has your details, ask support to check your account and, if needed, block your SIM.
