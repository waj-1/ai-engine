# Late Payment and Collections

## Overview

This article explains what happens at {{brand}} in the UAE and Saudi Arabia when a bill is not paid by its due date, and the options available to help you get back on track.

## Late fee

Bills are due 20 days after the bill date. If a bill is not paid by the due date, a late fee of 25 dirhams in the UAE or 25 riyals in Saudi Arabia is added.

## What happens as a balance stays overdue

| Days overdue | What happens |
|---|---|
| After the due date | A late fee of 25 dirhams or 25 riyals is added |
| 30 days | Service is restricted: home internet speed is reduced and outgoing calls are barred |
| 60 days | Service is suspended |

During restriction you can still receive calls and contact {{brand}} to arrange payment.

## Reconnection

Once the overdue balance is paid, service is restored. If service was suspended, a reconnection fee of 50 dirhams or 50 riyals applies.

## Promise to pay

If you cannot pay today, you can agree a promise to pay: a date up to 7 days away by which you will pay the full overdue balance. Keeping the promise avoids further collection steps for that period.

## Payment plan

If your overdue balance is 300 dirhams or 300 riyals or more, you can ask to split it into up to 3 monthly instalments. Each instalment must be paid on time to keep the plan active.

## Hardship support

If you are facing financial difficulty, tell {{brand}} early. The team will listen to your situation and go through the options above, such as a promise to pay or a payment plan, to find something that works for you.

## Payment links

- {{brand}} can send you a secure payment link to pay by card.
- Payment links are sent by SMS only, and only to the registered mobile number on the account.
- {{brand}} never sends payment links to any other number. If you receive a payment request from an unknown source, do not use it; pay through the app instead.

## Calls about an overdue balance

{{brand}} may call you about an overdue balance. Before discussing your account, the agent will ask you to confirm your date of birth or the last 4 digits of your national ID.

## If you have already paid

- Share your payment reference (format PAY-12345) with the agent or support.
- Bank transfers can take up to 2 working days to appear, so a recent transfer may not show yet.
- Once the payment is confirmed, the account is updated and any restriction is lifted.
