# Refunds and Disputes

## Overview

This article explains how {{brand}} handles duplicate payments and how to dispute a charge on your bill. It applies to customers in both the UAE and Saudi Arabia.

## Duplicate payments

If you paid the same bill twice, for example by card in the app and again by bank transfer, you can choose how to get the extra amount back:

- **Refund to the original payment method:** the money is returned to the card or bank account you paid from within 5 to 7 working days.
- **Bill credit:** the extra amount is applied immediately as a credit on your account and used against your next bill.

To request either option, contact support with the payment references (format PAY-12345) of both payments.

## Disputing a charge

If you believe a charge on your bill is wrong, you can dispute it within 60 days of the bill date.

To raise a dispute:

1. Contact {{brand}} support in the app or by phone.
2. Confirm your identity.
3. Tell support which bill and which line you are disputing, and why.
4. You receive confirmation that the dispute has been logged.

## What evidence helps

Disputes are reviewed faster when you provide:

- The bill date and the exact line or amount you are disputing.
- Payment references (PAY-12345) for any payments involved.
- Bank or card statements showing the payment.
- Screenshots or messages, for example an order confirmation or a message about a roaming pass or data top-up.
- Dates and times of any service problem related to the charge.

## Your account during a review

While a dispute is under review, your account stays in normal status. The disputed amount does not lead to a late fee, restriction or suspension during the review. Charges that are not disputed should still be paid by the due date.

## After the review

- If the dispute is accepted, the charge is corrected, usually as a credit on your account.
- If it is not accepted, support explains the reason and the amount becomes payable.

## Disputes after 60 days

Charges older than 60 days from the bill date are outside the dispute window. Contact support if you have questions about an older bill.
