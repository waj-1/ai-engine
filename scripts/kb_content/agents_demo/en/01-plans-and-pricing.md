# Plans and Pricing

## Overview

{{brand}} provides home internet, mobile and business connectivity in the UAE and Saudi Arabia. Prices are in UAE dirhams (AED) for customers in the UAE and Saudi riyals (SAR) for customers in Saudi Arabia. All prices in this article are per month.

## Home internet plans

| Plan | Speed | UAE (AED) | Saudi Arabia (SAR) | Contract |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Home Fiber 300 | 300 Mbps | 289 | 279 | 12 or 24 months |
| Home Fiber 500 | 500 Mbps | 349 | 339 | 12 or 24 months |
| Home Fiber 1 Gig | 1 Gbps | 449 | 429 | 12 or 24 months |
| Home 5G Wireless | up to 250 Mbps (typical) | 249 | 239 | No contract |

- **Home Fiber 300:** unlimited data and a Wi-Fi 6 router included. Best for couples and small households.
- **Home Fiber 500:** unlimited data and a Wi-Fi 6 router included. Best for families with several people streaming and working from home.
- **Home Fiber 1 Gig:** unlimited data, a Wi-Fi 6 router plus 2 mesh units for full-home coverage. Best for big households, gamers, 4K streaming and home offices.
- **Home 5G Wireless:** plug-and-play, no installation visit and no contract. Best for areas without fiber yet, renters, or anyone who wants internet the next working day.

## 24-month prices for home fiber

On a 24-month contract, each home fiber plan has a catalog price that is 20 less per month than the 12-month price:

| Plan | UAE 24-month (AED) | Saudi Arabia 24-month (SAR) |
|---|---|---|
| Home Fiber 300 | 269 | 259 |
| Home Fiber 500 | 329 | 319 |
| Home Fiber 1 Gig | 429 | 409 |

## Home add-on

- **Mesh Wi-Fi add-on:** one extra mesh unit to remove Wi-Fi dead spots, rented monthly. 25 dirhams in the UAE, 25 riyals in Saudi Arabia. No contract.

## Mobile plans

| Plan | What's included | UAE (AED) | Saudi Arabia (SAR) | Contract |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mobile Essential | 25 GB data, 500 local minutes | 99 | 89 | None or 12 months |
| Mobile Plus | 60 GB data, unlimited local calls, 2 GB GCC roaming | 179 | 169 | None or 12 months |
| Mobile Unlimited | Unlimited data (fair use 500 GB), unlimited local calls, 10 GB GCC roaming | 299 | 289 | None or 12 months |

- **Family line add-on:** an extra mobile line on the same account with 20 GB data and unlimited local calls. 49 dirhams or 45 riyals per line per month. Up to 4 extra lines, all on one bill. No contract.

## Business plans

{{brand}} also offers business fiber, an SME bundle and a 5G backup line for businesses. See the business bundles article for details.

## Phone financing

Any phone in the store can be paid over 12 or 24 months at 0%. For example, the Nova X Pro (a fictional model) costs 3,600 dirhams or 3,480 riyals, which is 150 dirhams or 145 riyals a month over 24 months. Phone financing is for customers in good standing.

## Ending a contract early

If a contract is ended before its term, an early termination charge of 50% of the remaining contract value applies.

## What {{brand}} does not offer

- TV / IPTV packages
- Landline-only phone service
- Student or senior discounts
- Satellite internet
