# Billing and Payments

## Overview

{{brand}} customers in the UAE are billed in dirhams (AED) and customers in Saudi Arabia in riyals (SAR). This article explains when bills arrive, how to read them and how to pay.

## Bill cycle and due date

- You receive one bill every month for all the services on your account, including any family lines.
- Each bill has a bill date. The bill is due 20 days after the bill date.
- Paying by the due date keeps your account in good standing and avoids a late fee.

## How to read your bill

Your bill lists each charge on its own line:

- **Account details:** account number, bill date, due date and billing period.
- **Previous balance and payments received:** what was owed on the last bill and any payments made since.
- **Monthly plan charges:** the monthly price of each plan, such as your home internet plan and mobile plan.
- **Add-ons:** for example a Mesh Wi-Fi add-on or family lines, each shown separately.
- **Phone financing:** the monthly instalment, if you are paying for a phone over 12 or 24 months.
- **Roaming passes and data top-ups:** each pass or top-up you bought appears as a separate line, so you can see exactly what was added.
- **One-time charges:** for example an installation fee or late fee, if any apply.
- **Total amount due:** the amount to pay by the due date.

## Payment methods

You can pay your bill by:

- **Card:** in the app, on the website, or through a secure payment link.
- **Bank transfer:** use your account number as the reference.
- **The app:** pay any amount at any time.
- **Auto-pay:** save a card and the bill is paid automatically each month.

## Payment references

Every payment gets a payment reference in the format PAY-12345. Keep this reference; support will ask for it if you need to check or query a payment.

## When payments appear

- Card and app payments normally show on your account straight away.
- A payment made by bank transfer can take up to 2 working days to appear on your account.

If you paid by bank transfer and the payment does not show yet, wait up to 2 working days before contacting support, and have your payment reference ready.

## Need help?

Contact {{brand}} support in the app or by phone if a line on your bill is unclear. If you believe a charge is wrong, see the article on refunds and disputes.
