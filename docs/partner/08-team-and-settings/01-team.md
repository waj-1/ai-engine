# Choose who can use the portal

**Team & access** lists the people who can sign in to this portal. Give someone access by making them an admin, and take it away the same way.

![The Team & access screen with the search box, the Make admin button, the Admin label and Show service accounts numbered](../media/team-and-settings/team.png)

## Open it

Click **Settings** at the bottom of the sidebar, then **Team & access**.

The list shows each person, their **Access** and when they were **Last active**. Under each name you may also see their role in the app the portal signs people in with.

## Give someone access

1. Find the person. Type part of their name, email or phone number in **Search people**.
2. Click **Make admin** on their row and confirm.
3. Their **Access** now shows **Admin** (3 in the screenshot). They can sign in straight away with their usual sign-in details.

Admins can change every agent and setting, so only give access to people who need it.

## Remove someone's access

1. Click **Remove admin** on their row and confirm.
2. Their **Access** changes to **No portal access**. They can't sign in to the portal any more. Any access they have to your other apps is not changed.

You can't remove your own admin access. Ask another admin to do it.

## Someone isn't in the list?

The list shows people who already have an account. If a colleague isn't there, ask your portal administrator to create their account first, then make them an admin here.

## Service accounts

4. **Show service accounts** adds the accounts used by automated systems and tests rather than people. Admins, including the one created on the first-run page, always show in the main list. You can usually leave them alone. Don't remove a service account's access unless your portal administrator asks you to.

## Every change is recorded

Each time someone gains or loses access, it's recorded in **Activity** with who made the change and when. See [See who changed what](02-activity.md).
