# Your first 15 minutes

Get to know one agent from start to finish: see what it does, try it yourself, then read how it handled a real customer.

<video src="../media/getting-started/first-15-minutes.mp4" controls muted playsinline width="100%"></video>
*From the Agents list to a real conversation in under a minute: open Lina, glance at her instructions, open the test chat, then read a conversation that needs attention.*

## 1. Look at an agent

1. Click **Agents** in the sidebar. Each row shows what the agent does, where people reach it (**Chat**, **Voice**), and whether it's **Active** or a **Draft**.
2. Click an agent's name. You land on its **Overview** tab:
   - **What this agent does**: its purpose and who talks to it.
   - **At a glance**: a preview of its **Instructions**, its **Tools & knowledge**, any **Sub-agents** and its **Voice**. Each card has a link to change it.
   - **Health**: its last test run and its conversations from the last 7 days.
3. Click the **Instructions** tab to read exactly what the agent is told before every conversation. You don't need to change anything yet.

## 2. Try it yourself

1. Click **Test** at the top of the agent's page. A chat panel opens on the right.
2. Type a message as a customer would, for example "My internet is slow tonight", and click **Send**.
3. Read the reply. Try a follow-up, or a question the agent shouldn't answer, to see how it copes.
4. Close the panel with **×** when you're done.

Test chats don't reach customers. See [Test your agent](../02-agents/08-test-your-agent.md) for more.

## 3. Read a real conversation

1. Go back to the **Overview** tab. Under **Health**, click **Last 7 days →** next to **Conversations**. You'll see this agent's conversations only.
2. Click any conversation in the list. It opens on the right.
3. If it's marked **Needs attention**, a red box at the top explains what went wrong. Click **Jump to message** to see the exact moment.

See [Review a conversation](../05-monitor/02-review-a-conversation.md) for the full guide.
