# Group your agents into categories

When you have many agents, **categories** keep them tidy. A category is a group you make yourself, such as **Support**, **Sales** or **Internal tools**. Each agent can be in one category, or in none.

A category only organises the list. It does **not** change what an agent says or does, and you don't need to publish anything after changing one.

A new portal starts with no categories. Add the ones that fit how your team thinks about its agents.

## Create a category

1. Open **Agents**, then the **Categories** tab.
2. Click **New category**.
3. Type a **Name**, for example "Support". A short **Description** helps your colleagues know what belongs there.
4. Pick a **Colour**. It's the colour of the category's label on the Agents list.
5. Click **Add category**.

You can usually leave **Advanced** as it is. The technical name in there is how an AI assistant refers to the category; it stays the same if you rename the category later.

## Put an agent in a category

**An agent you already have:**

1. Open the agent and go to **Settings**.
2. Choose the category in **Category**. Choose **— Uncategorised —** to take it out of its category.
3. Click **Save settings**.

**A new agent:** in the **New agent** window, step 2, choose a **Category** before you click **Create draft agent**. If you create a team, its sub-agents start in the same category. See [Create a new agent](07-new-agent.md).

A draft copy of an agent starts in the same category as the original.

## Find agents by category

On the **Agents** list, each agent in a category shows its coloured label next to its name.

- Use the **Any category** filter to see one category, or **Uncategorised** for the agents that have none.
- Typing a category's name in **Search agents** finds its agents too.
- On the **Categories** tab, the number in the **Agents** column opens the list already filtered to that category.

The filter appears once you have at least one category.

## Rename, recolour or reorder

On the **Categories** tab:

- **Edit** changes the name, description or colour. The agents in the category show the new name and colour straight away.
- The **↑** and **↓** buttons move a category up or down. That's the order used in the filter and in the pickers.

## Delete a category

1. On the **Categories** tab, click **Delete** next to the category.
2. The window tells you how many agents are in it. Read it, then click **Delete category**.

**Deleting a category never deletes an agent.** The agents that were in it stay exactly as they are and simply have no category. You can put them in another one from each agent's **Settings**.

## Good to know

- A category is the same in Staging and Production. Change an agent's category once and both show it; there is nothing to publish.
- Restoring an earlier version of an agent does not change its category.
- Templates don't have categories.
