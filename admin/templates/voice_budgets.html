How budgets are applied
  • Budgets are per tenant, not per model: moving a business to another realtime model does not change, reset or duplicate its budget.
  • The default comes from VOICE_LIVE_DAILY_SECONDS, read when the engine starts — changing it needs an engine restart. Budgets on this page apply as soon as you save.
  • If the usage database can't be reached, calls are allowed (the budget controls spend, not availability).
